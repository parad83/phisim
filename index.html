• following operators can be used: +, -, *, /, ^, (, ) as well as standard functions such as: exp, ln, sin, cos, tan, abs, sqrt;
  • use x for variables;
  • you can also use e\!
  • you can change the tick count for the whole graph as well as step sizes for the axis;
  • the solver and renderer do not work well with asymptotic functions as the vertices are drawn with GL.LINES;
  • raw equations are parsed with Pratt parsing and evalated with JS Math;
  • all [A-Za-z]+ will be parsed but only aforementioned functions and x and e will be evaluated.
